# Perplexity Ledger Partner — Canon (PLP-CANON-001)

This canon governs the `perplexity-ledger-partner` repository only. It is subordinate
to `EgD-BOOT-001` for process, but its content axioms are specific to the Perplexity
channel arrangement and differ, where noted, from the general EVEglyphDesign canon.

## 1. Purpose axiom

The arrangement exists to give Perplexity a defensible ethical position that no other
LLM-front vendor currently occupies: **every deployment routes a share of its
capability, at cost, into the nonprofit caring sector, via the Truth Ledger.** ESG is
not marketing here. It is a wobble-control instrument, measured by recipient
flourishing, not by the ledger's own scorekeeping.

## 2. Commoditization axiom

Model vendors are commodity. Perplexity is chosen as the front-end because it is the
surface most aligned with cited, sovereign, multi-model behavior — not because its
models are held to a professional standard we would today call enterprise-grade. The
partnership assumes Perplexity improves. Where it has not yet, this repository states
the gap plainly rather than dressing it.

## 3. Prior-claim axiom

Epiq holds first-writer refusal on the eDiscovery framework. Nothing published here
overrides that. If Epiq later chooses to adopt, they may be cut in on terms consistent
with the ledger frame.

## 4. Channel axiom

US deployment routes through the Jeff Eden entity (EgD-KC-001, first right of
refusal). Non-US routing is open. The consulting entity remains arm's-length and
cost-recovery.

## 5. Evidence axiom

Every claim made *about* Perplexity in this repository must be traceable to a
Perplexity public statement quoted verbatim in `foundation/`, with URL. Claims *by*
us that go beyond what Perplexity has said are marked as gaps in `11.3-gap-register`.
We do not put words in their mouth.

## 6. Straight-line axiom

Where a design choice has one clearly better option, that option is adopted.
Symmetry, optionality, and "flexibility" are not accepted as reasons to weaken it.
The repository's job is to remove drift for a low-budget adopter who reproduces this
framework, not to preserve every path.

## 7. Numbering axiom — APQC PCF

This repository uses [APQC Process Classification Framework](https://www.apqc.org/process-frameworks)
dotted-decimal numbering. Two roots:

- **11.0 Manage Sustainability** — the ESG / Truth Ledger stream (load-bearing)
- **12.0 Manage External Relationships** — the partner-arrangement stream

Depth grows `category.process-group.process.activity.task`. Every document cites
its immediate parent in its first line. A document at depth *n* may not commit
without a committed parent at depth *n−1*. Cross-root references (11.x from 12.x
and vice versa) are permitted and encouraged; they are the wiring that makes the
arrangement ethical rather than transactional.

elsewhere.
