# Perplexity Ledger Partner — Canon (PLP-CANON-001)

This canon governs the `perplexity-ledger-partner` repository only. It is subordinate
to `EgD-BOOT-001` for process, but its content axioms are specific to the Perplexity
channel arrangement and differ, where noted, from the general EVEglyphDesign canon.

## 1. Purpose axiom

The arrangement exists to give Perplexity a defensible ethical position that no other
LLM-front vendor currently occupies: **every deployment routes a share of its capability,
at cost, into the nonprofit caring sector, via the Truth Ledger.** ESG is not marketing
here. It is a wobble-control instrument, measured by recipient flourishing, not by the
ledger's own scorekeeping.

## 2. Commoditization axiom

Model vendors are commodity. Perplexity is chosen as the front-end because it is the
surface most aligned with cited, sovereign, multi-model behavior — not because its
models are held to a professional standard we would today call enterprise-grade. The
partnership assumes Perplexity improves. Where it has not yet, this repository states
the gap plainly rather than dressing it.

## 3. Prior-claim axiom

Epiq holds first-writer refusal on the eDiscovery framework. Nothing published here
overrides that. If Epiq later chooses to adopt, they may be cut in on terms consistent
with the ledger frame.

## 4. Channel axiom

US deployment routes through the Jeff Eden entity (EgD-KC-001, first right of refusal).
Non-US routing is open. The consulting entity remains arm's-length and cost-recovery.

## 5. Evidence axiom

Every claim made *about* Perplexity in this repository must be traceable to a Perplexity
public statement quoted verbatim in `foundation/`, with URL. Claims *by* us that go
beyond what Perplexity has said are marked as gaps in `00-blueprint/03-gap-statement`.
We do not put words in their mouth.

## 6. Straight-line axiom

Where a design choice has one clearly better option, that option is adopted. Symmetry,
optionality, and "flexibility" are not accepted as reasons to weaken it. The
repository's job is to remove drift for a low-budget adopter who reproduces this
framework, not to preserve every path.

## 7. Numbering axiom

`00-` blueprint precedes `10-` technical precedes `20-` execution. `30-` tracking hangs
off `10-`. A downstream commit that cannot cite its upstream anchor is a defect.

## 8. Deltas from the general EVEglyphDesign canon

|---|---|---|
| Front-end | Model-agnostic, cited sources required | Perplexity-first, other models via Perplexity orchestration |
