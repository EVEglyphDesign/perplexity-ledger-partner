Perplexity as ledger-front for the EVEglyphDesign eDiscovery framework

A channel-partner shape that gives Perplexity a defensible ethical position no other LLM-front vendor currently occupies: every deployment routes a share of its capability, at cost, into the nonprofit caring sector — via the Truth Ledger.

Status. Framing draft. Nothing here binds Epiq or Perplexity.
Prior claim. Epiq holds first-writer refusal on the eDiscovery framework and is being offered the chance to adopt it before any external channel is engaged.
US deployment. Any US-side execution routes through the Jeff Eden entity under EgD-KC-001 first right of refusal.

The arrangement, in one picture

The client engages through the Perplexity surface they already know. The framework runs behind it. A defined cost share of every engagement is booked into the Truth Ledger and distributed, at cost, to nonprofit caring-sector recipients. The distribution is evidenced on the ledger, not asserted in marketing.

Why Perplexity, not a hyperscaler front

  • Model vendors are commodity. Perplexity is the surface most aligned with cited, sovereign, multi-model behaviour.
  • Ethical positioning is available. No LLM-front vendor currently ties deployment revenue to caring-sector distribution by construction. The seat is empty.
  • Straight-line design. One better option per choice, adopted. No optionality kept for its own sake.

The ESG frame — Truth Ledger as wobble-control

Truth Ledger's load-bearing purpose is wobble-control: routing a share of every new high-tech capability, at cost, into the nonprofit caring sector so that the sector caring for churn casualties keeps pace with the technology causing them. Success is measured by recipient flourishing, not by the ledger's own scorekeeping.

Pour le bien-être du peuple is the whole purpose, not merely a closing phrase.
